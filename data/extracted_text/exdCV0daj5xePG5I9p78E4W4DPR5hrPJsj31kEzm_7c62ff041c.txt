===== PAGE 5 =====

Abstract
This final year thesis is a crucial part of the requirements for obtaining the National Bach-
elor’s Degree in Computer Engineering: IoT & Embedded Systems from the Higher Institute
of Computer Science of Mahdia. This project was created by me and proposed to the ABBK
enterprise, which provided the necessary guidance and mentorship throughout its development
phases.
The project’s primary objective is to develop a hackathon platform designed to streamline
the organization and management of hackathons, challenges, and competitions for the tech
community. This platform enables participants to register and submit their projects, organizers
to create and manage events, and judges to evaluate and score submissions. It also includes a
48-hour live featured hackathon powered with a strong anti-cheat AI system.
To ensure a scalable and practical solution, the platform leverages cutting-edge technologies
including Java Spring Boot for the backend (based on microservices architecture), Angular for
the frontend, and WPF for the admin desktop application. Additionally, AI & ML techniques
are integrated to develop the anti-cheat system, and cybersecurity techniques are integrated
into the RBAC authentication system (Spring Security, JWT, etc.).
An Agile methodology was adopted to facilitate iterative development and adaptability.
UML (Unified Modeling Language) was used in each sprint to ensure a structured and clear
representation of the system’s architecture.
Keywords: Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Angular, WPF, JWT, Agile, UML, Microservices,
Cybersecurity, Anti-cheat AI.

In an era where technological advances continuously redefine the standards of innovation and
collaboration, the global demand for platforms that empower IT professionals to showcase
their talents on a worldwide scale has never been greater. **IdeaPo** emerges as an innovative
solution and a powerful tool for companies seeking top talent.
This project, carried out in collaboration with the **ABBK Company** (formerly known

collaboration through hackathons, challenges, and team-based projects.
At its core, the project fosters both teamwork and competition, encouraging participants
to tackle mid- to high-complexity problems under pressure.
This is exemplified in its 48-
hour live hackathon feature, where students engage in intensive collaborative problem-solving.

preparing participants for real-world challenges.

sources: booking rooms, logistics, welcoming participants, coordination between
members, and using disparate tools to manage registrations, communication and
project submissions. For university clubs and student associations, this represents

they are. On the corporate side, IdeaPo offers a strategic opportunity to organise

to the winners - creating a win-win environment.

Identification of the actors :

with teammates or organizers.
• Judge: Reviews and evaluates participant submissions based on predefined

ranking and results.

Final Conclusion
As part of my final year project, I designed and developed an application
for a hackathon management platform. This report has outlined all
the stages I went through to achieve this goal, ultimately resulting in a
functional, complete, and deliverable product.
During the development lifecycle, I adopted the SCRUM methodology,
which allowed me to build the system incrementally. I started by analyzing
the overall context of the project and identifying the functional and
non-functional requirements. Once the scope was clear, I established a
prioritized work schedule to guide each sprint.
I then proceeded to the design and implementation phases, organizing
the work into well-defined sprints. Regular meetings with my SCRUM
master helped validate deliverables and review progress, ensuring
continuous improvement and quality control.
Despite various technical challenges and time constraints, I successfully
completed each sprint on time. The system adheres to important security
principles and is well-documented to support future development.
This project has been a highly enriching experience, offering practical
exposure beyond traditional academic work. It strengthened my knowledge
of modern web development using Angular and Spring Boot, and
provided valuable insight into collaborative software engineering.
Although the current version includes a comprehensive set of core features,
several enhancements remain possible. Notably, the integration of an
advanced anti-cheat detection system and an intelligent chatbot
module for automated client support are promising directions for future
work.
