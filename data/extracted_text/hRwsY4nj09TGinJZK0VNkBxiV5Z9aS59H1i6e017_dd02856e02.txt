Ce document se réfère à notre stage final au sein du Groupe Sindibad pour notre licence en Ingénierie

Le projet crée un système embarqué pour enregistrer l'entrée des employés en utilisant des badges

Une application web permet aux administrateurs et aux leaders de segment de vérifier la présence, de

Abstract : 
This document concerns our final internship at Sindibad Group for our Bachelor's degree in Computer 
Systems Engineering, specialising in IoT and Embedded Systems.  
The project creates an embedded system to clock employees in using NFC badges and real-time GPS 
geolocation. The objective is to modernise and automate the tracking of personnel transported by bus.  
A web application allows administrators and segment leaders to check attendance, filter data, track 
buses on a map, and export reports.  
The system is based on an IoT architecture connected via MQTT and deployed on Render. This work 
highlights the crucial role of connected technologies in the digitalisation of human resource 
management.
