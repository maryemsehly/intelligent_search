Abstract
This project presents an automated system for generating courses and exams, aimed at im-
proving efficiency and interactivity in education. By creating quizzes from a question bank,
it reduces manual effort and errors. The platform supports multiple user roles and provides
access to course materials and assessments, promoting a scalable, secure, and student-centered
learning environment.
Keywords: Automated Learning System, Exam Generation, Course Management, Academic
Content Automation, Intelligent Education Tools.
Résumé
Ce projet présente un système automatisé de génération de cours et d’examens, visant à améliorer
l’efficacité et l’interactivité dans le domaine de l’enseignement. En générant des quiz à partir
d’une banque de questions, il réduit les efforts manuels et les erreurs. La plateforme prend en
charge plusieurs types d’utilisateurs et offre un accès aux supports de cours et aux évaluations,
favorisant ainsi un environnement d’apprentissage évolutif, sécurisé et centré sur l’étudiant.
Mot-Clés: Système d’apprentissage automatisé, Génération d’examens, Gestion de cours,
Automatisation des contenus pédagogiques, Outils éducatifs intelligents..

GENERAL INTRODUCTION
I
n the rapidly evolving landscape of modern education, the demand for innovative, scalable,
and intelligent assessment tools has become increasingly vital.
Traditional methods of course delivery and exam preparation often struggle to keep pace with
the growing expectations of digital learning environments [1] [2].
In this context, automation and interactivity are no longer luxuries—they are necessities for
improving the quality and efficiency of educational processes.
This project responds to that challenge by proposing the development of an Automated
Course and Exam Generation System.
Its primary objective is to streamline the creation and management of learning materials and
evaluations, thereby reducing the repetitive manual workload typically faced by educators.
By generating quizzes and exams automatically from a structured question bank, the system
minimizes human error and maximizes time efficiency, allowing instructors to concentrate on
pedagogy and content refinement.
This work demonstrates how artificial intelligence can support education by enabling smarter
automation, adaptive content delivery, and improved decision-making for educators.
It opens a promising path toward more personalized, data-driven learning experiences powered
by intelligent systems [3] [4].
Beyond simplifying administrative tasks, the system enhances the student experience through
intuitive access to downloadable course materials and interactive assessments available both on-
line and offline.
It accommodates a variety of user roles, including educators, learners, and administrators,
offering each one tailored functionalities such as content management, participation in quizzes,
and system supervision.
By promoting academic integrity, supporting pedagogical adaptability, and ensuring oper-
ational scalability, this platform contributes to a modern, reliable, and engaging educational
ecosystem.
It is conceived to support a data-driven approach to teaching and learning, fostering acces-
sibility and efficiency for all stakeholders.

1.1
Introduction
This project aims to develop an AI-powered automated exam generation system capable of
creating customized assessments based on the subject matter, academic level, and recently
covered course content.
By leveraging large language models (LLMs) and semantic search
techniques, the platform seeks to streamline the exam creation process for educators while
ensuring pedagogical relevance and adaptability.

Architecture Used
We chose the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture (fig 1.5), a widely adopted design
pattern that separates an application into three core components: the Model (data and busi-
ness logic), the View (user interface), and the Controller (input handling). This separation
enhances code clarity, simplifies maintenance, and supports scalability throughout the devel-
opment lifecycle [9].

Model: The Model is responsible for managing the application’s data, business logic, and
rules. It defines how data is structured and behaves, handles validation and transformations,
and communicates with databases or external services when needed. Decoupled from the user
interface, the Model can be developed, tested, and maintained independently, promoting mod-
ularity and robustness.
View : The View is responsible for presenting data to the user in a clear, interactive,
and user-friendly manner. It encompasses all visual elements of the interface—such as but-
tons, forms, tables, and feedback indicators. The View obtains data from the Model via the
Controller and updates dynamically in response to changes. Designed for responsiveness and
usability, it plays a vital role in shaping the overall user experience.
Controller : The Controller serves as the link between the Model and the View. It in-
terprets user inputs, such as clicks or form submissions, and determines the appropriate re-
sponse—updating the Model and refreshing the View as needed. By managing application
logic and control flow, the Controller ensures smooth coordination between data and interface,
making it a key component in maintaining a responsive and interactive user experience.

GENERAL CONCLUSION
In conclusion, this project represents a significant advancement in the digital transformation of
education through the automation of academic content management.
By unifying course delivery, exam creation, and performance monitoring into a single, co-
herent system, it effectively addresses the growing demands for scalability, flexibility, and in-
teractivity in modern learning environments.
The developed platform reduces the administrative burden on instructors by automating
repetitive tasks such as quiz generation, grading, and resource sharing.
At the same time, it improves the student experience through immediate feedback, intuitive
navigation, and offline access to materials—encouraging autonomy and active learning.
For administrators, the system ensures structured monitoring and secure user management,
contributing to greater institutional efficiency and academic integrity.
From a technical standpoint, the successful integration of essential features—such as role-
based access, timer-based assessments, and dynamic content management—demonstrates the
robustness and scalability of the chosen architecture.
The modular design also ensures that the system can easily evolve to meet future educational
and technological challenges.
Perspectives: While the current implementation meets the core objectives, several promis-
ing directions can be pursued to further enhance the platform:
-AI-Powered Personalization: Integrating machine learning to recommend tailored content
based on student performance and learning behavior.
-Adaptive Testing: Implementing dynamic difficulty adjustment during assessments to bet-
ter evaluate individual competencies.
-Analytics Dashboard: Adding advanced visual analytics to help instructors track progress
trends and identify at-risk learners.
-Mobile Application: Extending the system to Android/iOS platforms for greater accessibil-
ity and real-time interaction.
