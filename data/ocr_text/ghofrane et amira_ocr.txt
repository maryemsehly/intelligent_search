1.5. Methodology 
The methodology is necessary for the management of any type of project. We choose the 
most adopted method,  ‎عطSCRUM method, which makes possible to manage work more 
securely, and to facilitate the design and development task. 
1.5.1. 
Methodology Scrum 
The Scrum method is a project management framework, which appeared in 1986 by 
“Hirotaka Takeuchi” and “Ikujiro Nonaka”. The objective is to improve the productivity of the 
teams by remaining ready to reorient the project measurement of its progress. This method 
facilitates  ‎عطplanning of  ‎عطproject in the duration, it relies عط
 ‎نهdecoupage of  ‎عطproject in 
“time boxes” called sprints. These have a fixed duration, the advances made between two 
sprints help to give an idea of  ‎عطteam’s rhythm. The Scrum methodology also requires a team 
multidisciplinary and autonomous [5]. 
1.5.2. 
The roles 
The Scrum team has three main roles [5]: 
e 
Product owner: he is the project manager. He works in interaction with the 
development team by defining the priority of the features and keeping the “product 
backlog” up to date. 
e 
Scrum master: he is the person responsible for understanding, adhering to and 
organizing the Scrum methodology. He holds the role of coach for development teams 
by improving communication within the team and seeks to maximize its productivity. 
e 
Development team: they are persons who realize the work of transforming needs into 
usable functionalities. The team size varies between 3 and 10 persons. They together 
make all necessary decisions. 
1.5.3. 
Scrum process 
The life of a Scrum project consists of clearly defined and limited meetings: 
e 
Sprint Planning: The development team chooses the priority elements of the 
"Product Backlog" during  ‎عطmeeting that it is able  ‎مachieve during this sprint in 
agreement with "Product Owner [S]".

e 
Sprint Review: Once the sprint  ‎قلover,  ‎عطteam presents the full features and receives 
feedback from  ‎"عطProduct Owner". At this point, a new iteration cycle begins [5]. 
e 
Sprint Retrospective: The retrospective between the review of  ‎عطsprint completed 
and the planning of the next sprint is an opportunity to improve productivity, quality, 
efficiency, etc. 
e 
Daily Scrum: This meeting takes up  ‎ما15 minutes to take stock of the sprint’s daily
