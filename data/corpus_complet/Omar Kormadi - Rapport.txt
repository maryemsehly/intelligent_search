GENERAL INTRODUCTION
Nowadays, we can notice the vast amount of poverty in Tunisia, and the lack of basics
necessities for a decent existence, such as clothes, money, home supplies and educational
needs, we would say it remains a problem, but not an issue, Nobody supports it and we
have a lot of resources to get it done. Where it exists, it is the result of war, crimes or
failing government, insanity, or other problems. There’s no single anti-poverty solution,
we have to deal with the different issues that cause this type of poverty, also people can’t
donate easily even if they want to donate because they don’t know any association or
organization which will help them to make them donate.
In this context, our final study project, will be to design and create a mobile application
that will cover part of this problem, our application will facilitate the operations of
donations between donors and associations through the contact that will happen in the
application between both of them in order to make people in need receive all the basic
necessities chosen by the donor in the application.
This report is divided into six chapters:
• In the first chapter, we begin by introducing the host organization and the general
framework of the project where we introduce the study of the existing, the critical,
and then the proposed solutions. Also, present the methodology design adopted for
the realization of our project.
• The second chapter is devoted to situating functional and describing the technical
study while specifying the technologies and the tools used.
• Chapters Three, Four, and Five are dedicated to presenting respectively sprints
developed according to the SCRUM methodology. Each Sprint describes the pur-
pose of the sprint, the needs analysis, the conceptual modeling, the realization, and
tests.

• The sixth chapter is committed to talk about the recommendation system in our
project.
We conclude this report with a conclusion, through which we summarize everything that
has been done throughout the work of our project and presents the added value of the
project by opening up new perspectives.

The team involved and its relationships with the customer are at the heart of XP. The XP
method is based on flexible responsiveness to changing customer needs, teamwork, and
quality of work. For this method to be effective the eXtreme Programming methodology
requires a change of mentality by all the teams involved.
It is therefore a question of applying extremely the principles of agile development, it may

The ”RUP” method

In reality, it is a long and detailed process that requires complete documentation can be
discouraging.
This methodology, therefore, requires a significant investment cost, and cannot be con-
sidered agile for its rigidity.
This method focuses on four phases of evolution that make up the RUP:
• Creation: Planning and design.
• Development: Requirement specification.

2.4.3.2
n-Tier Architecture
Our application is a Flutter application with Firebase corresponding to the first two
layers: the presentation layer (UI) and the business logic layer (Business Logic). Firebase
acts as the data access layer, facilitating communication with the remote database server
via the Internet. The web server layer is managed by Firebase services, which take care
of the secure communication with the database server. Hence the architecture of our
application at 3 levels (3-tier architecture). The three-tier or three-layer architecture is
a client-server architecture in which independent modules coexist and are maintained to
render a User Interface (GUI), logical, functional, and business processes as well as data
access. Indeed, any application can be divided into three parts: a graphic interface part,
a functional part, and a data storage part. And it is to these precise needs that the
three-tier architecture was designed by cutting three distinct parts:
Figure 4.3.11: Hardware architecture of the application
1-Presentation Tier (UI):
-This layer corresponds to the user interface of our Flutter application. -It includes
the widgets, screens and graphical components used to display and interact with
the application. -In a Flutter application with Firebase, Flutter widgets play a
central role in this layer, handling the display and capture of user input.
2- Application Tier:
-This layer manages the business logic of our application. - It handles the rules
and operations specific to our application domain. - In a Flutter application with
Firebase, this layer can include state management, data processing, and application
logic classes. - We can use libraries such as BLoC (Business Logic Component) or
Provider to manage the business logic in this layer.
20


===== PAGE 33 =====

3- Data Tier:
- This layer is responsible for data access, including communication with the re-
mote database server via the Internet. -Firebase plays the role of the data access
layer in this architecture. -Firebase provides services such as Firestore (database),
Authentication (user authentication), Storage (file storage), etc. - We can use the
oﬀicial Firebase packages for Flutter to interact with Firebase from this layer and
perform data read, write and update operations.
The three-tier architecture is modern and its division into three parts makes it essential
for the development of mobile applications, in order to provide more performance and
security but also provide easier maintenance.
It is opposed to the traditional 2-tier
infrastructure which does not separate the application server part from the database part
(both are on the same logical support).

GENERAL CONCLUSION
Our project entitled ”CONCEPTION AND REALIZATION OF A DONATION MO-
BILE APPLICATION WASIT” consists of the conception and realization of a mobile
application, intended to allow donors to donate through associations.
We did notice that in our country we don’t have that advanced technology that encourages
people to donate from their homes, that’s why we realized a system that allows people
to donate to poor people and make contact with associations to receive their donations
from their home and via our mobile application.
Regarding the approach, we first proceeded to a study phase of the various non-existent
tools. Secondly, we specified our application to discern the functionality. Thirdly, we
proceeded to its design as well as to the technological choices for its realization. Finally,
we have implemented.
All functionalities described in the functional specifications have been developed and
validated.
In today’s interconnected world, mobile applications have emerged as powerful tools for
driving positive change. One specific category of mobile apps that has gained signifi-
cant attention is donation applications. These innovative platforms aim to facilitate and
streamline the process of charitable giving, empowering individuals and organizations to
make a meaningful impact, indeed if we would like to take a look at the perspectives of
our app, we will add a lot of details, but the main ones are:
-Future Innovations which means advancements in technology, such as Artificial
intelligence, can enhance transparency and traceability in the verification of the
real associations that exist in Tunisia. Indeed the process of this method is going
to be through giving the association evidence when he is going to register, the

belongs to our country or not, if he belongs, the operation of the registration will
be continued if he is a scam the operation will be stopped, further bolstering trust

-Collaboration: partnerships between donation apps and other sectors, such as
corporations and social media platforms (JUMIA, AZIZA, Carrefour), can amplify
the reach and impact of charitable initiatives. These collaborations can lead to
creative fundraising campaigns, viral challenges, and increased public awareness of
social causes. Moreover, these partnerships will be added as items in our app in

By amplifying charitable giving, democratizing philanthropy, and promoting transparency

challenges and leveraging future innovations, donation apps have the potential to create
