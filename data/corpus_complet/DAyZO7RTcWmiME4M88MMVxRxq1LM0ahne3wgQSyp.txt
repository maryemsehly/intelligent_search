Customer satisfaction represents a critical success factor for organizations operating in
today’s competitive business environment. Modern consumers demand rapid, effective
solutions to their concerns, transforming complaint management from a reactive function into
a strategic instrument for customer retention and loyalty building.
Technological advancement is reshaping customer service delivery through intelligent
automation systems, particularly chatbots, which provide immediate responses to routine
inquiries while routing complex issues to qualified personnel. This approach significantly
improves response times and service quality.

immediate assistance. Technical personnel can process complaints and escalate complex cases
to supervisors, who manage task distribution and team coordination. Management staff access
analytical dashboards to support informed decision-making.
This integrated solution establishes an optimized workflow that leverages data analytics
for continuous improvement. The platform provides intuitive user access while ensuring
complete complaint traceability throughout the resolution cycle. The automated chatbot
manages routine support functions, enabling technical teams to concentrate on complex cases
while delivering immediate customer assistance.

1.5.2.1
EVENTS
Scrum incorporates four structured events for inspection and adaptation, all contained within
the Sprint cycle.
Sprint Planning: Launches each Sprint by defining the work to be completed. The entire
Scrum Team collaborates to create this plan, with the Product Owner ensuring discussion of the
most critical Product Backlog items and their alignment with the Product Goal.
• Daily Scrum: A 15-minute daily meeting for Developers to review Sprint Goal progress
and adjust the Sprint Backlog accordingly. Held consistently at the same time and location
each working day to maintain simplicity and routine.
• Sprint Review:Examines Sprint outcomes and identifies future adjustments. The Scrum
Team demonstrates their work to key stakeholders, discussing Product Goal progress.

Participants review Sprint accomplishments and environmental changes to collaboratively

• Sprint Retrospective:Focuses on improving quality and effectiveness by analyzing the
previous Sprint’s performance regarding team dynamics, interactions, processes, tools,
and Definition of Done adherence.
1.5.2.2
ARTIFACTS
Scrum’s artifacts represent work or value. They are designed to maximize transparency of key
information. Thus, everyone inspecting them has the same basis for adaptation.
• Product Backlog: The Product Backlog is an emergent, ordered list of what is needed to
improve the product. It is the single source of work undertaken by the Scrum Team.
• Sprint Backlog: The Sprint Backlog is composed of the Sprint Goal (why), the set of
Product Backlog items selected for the Sprint (what), as well as an actionable plan for
delivering the Increment (how). is a plan by and for the Developers.
• Increment:An Increment is a concrete stepping stone toward the Product Goal. Each
Increment is additive to all prior Increments and thoroughly verified, ensuring that all
Increments work together. In order to provide value, the Increment must be usable.

NON-FUNCTIONAL NEEDS
Non-functional requirements describe the operational constraints and qualities our website must
meet to function properly.
Here are the key non-functional requirements for our website:
• Security: The website must protect data confidentiality with strong authentication and
appropriate protection for sensitive customer complaint information.
• Availability: The website needs to be accessible to users at all times, with high uptime
and minimal interruptions to ensure continuous service.
• Usability: All interfaces must be simple, easy to use, and intuitive to navigate, providing
a logical flow and optimal user experience for everyone.
• Reliability: The data provided by the website must be accurate and relevant, maintaining
consistency across all modules of the complaint management system.
• Integrity: We must ensure data integrity and consistency with every update and new entry,
using proper validation to maintain information quality.
• Performance: The system’s response and loading times must be fast, even under heavy
user load. This is especially critical for the real-time chatbot.
• Portability and Compatibility: The system should work well across different platforms
and modern web browsers to ensure universal access.
• Scalability: Our website needs to maintain its performance even as the number of complaints
and users grows, ensuring it can adapt to future demand.

GENERAL CONCLUSION
At Tunisie Telecom, we have engaged in a project aimed at modernizing and optimizing an
essential process for improving customer satisfaction: complaints management. This report
reflects our dedicated work in developing a comprehensive web platform integrating chatbot
technology for this specific task.
We began our report by first presenting the host company Tunisie Telecom and its PMO
department. We then conducted a critical evaluation of the existing complaints management
system and proposed our "TTClientCare" solution. In addition, we outlined the Scrum methodology
adopted for effective project management.
The project was then divided into sprints, each forming a separate chapter of our report.
We started with sprint zero, where we established a product backlog and defined the overall
architecture. The functionalities listed in this backlog were then divided into three main sprints:
the first sprint focused on the basic infrastructure and authentication, the second sprint dedicated
to the claims processing workflow, and the third sprint focused on the integration of artificial
intelligence and analytical dashboards.
For each sprint, we developed the design with UML diagrams, carried out the development
and performed the tests, providing screenshots and detailed descriptions.
This internship has been an extremely enriching experience, immersing us in the heart of
modern software development and giving us a better understanding of the challenges of digital
transformation in the telecoms sector. The development of the website described in this report
offered us a unique opportunity to discover and master many cutting-edge technologies.

experience enabled us to gain real professional experience, confronting us with responsibility,
