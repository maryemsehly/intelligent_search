General introduction
In today’s modern world, where health and ﬁtness play a vital role in our lives, we understand
the importance of making gym services more accessible and convenient. That’s why we have
developed a state-of-the-art gym app designed to revolutionize the way people engage with
ﬁtness centers.
Our gym app serves as a comprehensive platform, offering a range of features to streamline and
enhance the gym experience. With our app, users can easily access a variety of services and
resources, making their ﬁtness journey more enjoyable and efﬁcient.
One of the key features of our app is the ability to browse and book ﬁtness courses. Users
can explore a diverse range of classes and reserve their spots with ease, ensuring they can
participate in their preferred workouts at their preferred times.
Additionally, our app allows users to reserve gym equipment, eliminating the need to wait
for machines or weights to become available. By providing a simple and efﬁcient equipment
reservation system, users can optimize their workout routines and make the most of their time
at the gym.
Furthermore, our app includes subscription management capabilities, enabling users to easily
manage their gym memberships, make payments, and track their subscription status, all in one
place.
To facilitate smooth operations and efﬁcient management, our gym app also offers an admin
dashboard for gym administrators. This feature allows them to monitor important metrics, track
user activities, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall gym experience.
By leveraging the power of technology, our gym app aims to simplify and improve the way
people engage with ﬁtness centers. We believe that everyone should have access to convenient
and efﬁcient gym services, and our app provides a user-friendly solution to achieve this goal.

Why Scrum
Initially, our project idea lacked clarity, leaving us uncertain about which aspects to prioritize,
postpone, retain, or eliminate. To address this challenge, we opted for the SCRUM methodol-
ogy, believing that it would assist us in effectively organizing our priorities. Moreover, SCRUM
offers numerous advantages that align with our project goals, including:
1. Scrum can help teams complete project deliverables quickly and efﬁciently.
2. Scrum ensures effective use of time and money.
3. Large projects are divided into easily manageable sprints.
4. Developments are coded and tested during the sprint review.
5. Works well for fast-moving development projects.
6. The team gets clear visibility through scrum meetings.
7. Scrum, being agile, adopts feedback from customers and stakeholders.
8. Short sprints enable changes based on feedback a lot more easily.
9. The individual effort of each team member is visible during daily scrum meetings.
By adopting the SCRUM methodology, we aim to leverage these advantages to drive our project
towards success [1].

1.6.3
Scrum team
During the project’s development phase, the SCRUM team commences each workday with a
15-minute meeting known as the daily scrum. This meeting serves the purpose of synchronizing
activities, discussing challenges or issues, clarifying project-related matters, and more. Within
the SCRUM cycle, each team member holds a distinct role:
1. The Product Owner: This individual owns the project and is responsible for determining
what is desired and why. They oversee the management of the product backlog.
2. The Scrum Master: Acting as a process facilitator, the Scrum Master manages the SCRUM
process and works to remove any impediments that may arise.
3. Development Team: This team takes charge of delivering the project and operates in a
self-organized manner.

System architecture
The application process is based on a service-oriented architecture as follows: The data ex-
change between the back-end API and the web API is done using the modern http protocol, the
same for the front-end part, the data is in JSON format. Systems that follow the principles of
the REST architecture are often called RESTful.
• Web service: A web service is a resource that is available over the internet. It’s valuable
because it provides functionality other applications can use, such as payment process-
ing, logins, and database storage. This collection of protocols and standards is typically
used to exchange data between apps or systems. There are two major categories of web
service: SOAP and REST.[20]
• Rest API: A REST API (also known as RESTful API) is an application programming
interface (API or web API) that conforms to the constraints of REST architectural style
and allows for interaction with RESTful web services. REST stands for representational
state transfer and was created by computer scientist Roy Fielding.[21]

General Conclusion
During our internship at Move Up, we had the opportunity to apply the theoretical knowledge
we gained during our academic studies. Our primary focus was on front-end development,
where we dedicated a signiﬁcant amount of time to creating user interfaces. Through multiple
iterations, we reﬁned the design and functionality, although we recognize that there is still room
for improvement in the current version.
One of the highlights of this internship was the opportunity to work on the entire project, han-
dling both the back-end and front-end development and seamlessly integrating them. Although
we initially planned to explore the complexity of the project and the limited timeframe posed
challenges, we prioritized other aspects. However, with the guidance and support of our men-
tors at Move Up, we successfully managed the project independently.
We found immense satisfaction in bringing ideas to life, starting from prototypes and trans-
forming them into functional code. The approval and satisfaction of our supervisor at Move Up
further validated our efforts.
This internship at Move Up has been a valuable learning experience, allowing us to apply
theoretical knowledge and enhance our skills in web and mobile application development.
Additionally, we gained valuable insights into project management, which will undoubtedly
contribute to our future endeavors.
Finally, it is important to note that our current project version is a preliminary iteration, as it
is still under development. We understand that there are areas that require further optimization
and reﬁnement. In the future, we plan to dedicate additional effort to enhance the functional-
ity, performance, and user experience of the application. Our aim is to create a polished and
efﬁcient ﬁnal product that meets the needs and expectations of our users. By continually iterat-
ing and incorporating user feedback, we are conﬁdent that the project will evolve into a more
advanced and optimized solution.
