General Introduction
In the last few years mobile app development has took over the world by storm and
became a booming industry. That’s why it became clear to many companies that mo-
bile app development is a key factor for business success. It is with this in mind that
Sindibad Group have decided to offer innovative and fast mobile application services to
its customers.
This graduation project is being conducted in the context of creating a mobile appli-
cation that allows the employer to track the presence of his employees, as well as send
geographical data of the vehicles that helps the employees track their location.
This project will solve the two main problems are facing leoni, one of Sindbad group’s
clients, which are the major issue of intruders that are using the company buses as a free
way of transportation and the many drivers that always arrive late and are driving the
vehicles for personal use.
we will present the different phases of this project in five distinct chapters:
• Chapter I, presents the general context of the project and the chosen work method-
ology. It gives a general overview of the host organisation and a study of the existing
solutions.
• Chapter II, details the requirements analysis and specifications.
• Chapter III, presents the first sprint which is dedicated to the Authentication and
General Management.

1.3.1
Scope
The main reason for the creation of this project is to obtain the License in Computer
Science from the Higher Institute of Computer Science of Mahdia (ISIMa). In this project,
I developed an application to manage the attendance of the employees and track the routes
taken by vehicles.
1.3.2
Project Presentation
The purpose of the project is to create a mobile application where users can track the
attendance of the employees by scanning their NFC tokens. The application will need to
register administrators, employees, drivers, vehicles and routes and to allow to share the
location data.
We also created a web application that is primarily an administrative dashboard that
provides administrators with the tools necessary for faster and more convenient managerial
tasks.
1.3.3
Study of The Existing Solutions
There isn’t currently an existing solution in the market that satisfies our client’s exact
needs.
We’ve tried various methods to complete the task required but only one was
efficient.
In this part, we will present the different methods we’ve tried.
• Barcode Scanning: We’ve integrated barcode scanning into our app as a way
to scan the barcodes that are printed on the back of the tokens as a way to track
attendance
Advantages:
– Easy to use.
Disadvantages:

1.3.4
Proposed Solution
• NFC: Near-field communication(NFC) is a technology that allows two electronic
devices to communicate at close range. Each token is equipped with an NFC chip
that can be scanned without any of the inconveniences of the other methods.
Advantages:
– Extremely fast.
– Accurate.
